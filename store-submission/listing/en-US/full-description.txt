Summa is a sheet of paper that does the maths. Write a line in plain words and the answer appears next to it, on the right. Change a number and everything that depends on it updates.

Words around numbers are fine
Lunch: $12 + 15% tip
Flights: 2 × €149
Hotel: 3 nights × $165 in EUR
Summa skips the words it doesn't know and works out the rest.

Units and money
5 km in miles, 72 °F in °C, 1,200 sq ft in m², 6.4 L/100 km in mpg, $25/hour × 14 hours. Live exchange rates for 166 currencies and metals, refreshed at most twice a day, with rates built in for offline use. Crypto prices if you turn them on.

Percentages, every way
20% of $10, 5% on $30, 6% off €40, $50 as a % of $100, 20 is 10% of what.

Dates, times and time zones
days until Dec 25, next friday + 2 weeks, 8:30 am + 3 h 20 min, 3 pm Munich in Tokyo, workdays until Dec 18, next holiday. Over 6,000 cities and the usual abbreviations, all offline. Workday maths skips the public holidays of your region (22 countries).

Money over time
$10,000 at 5% for 10 years compounded monthly, loan of $300k at 6% for 30 years (the monthly payment), €1,350 incl 19% VAT, €120 without 20% VAT.

Your own names, units and functions
rate = $85/hour, then 6.5 hours × rate. Refer to a line with line6, the line above with prev, and add up a block with sum. Define 1 coffee = $4.50 or tip(bill) = bill × 18% once on the Definitions sheet and use them in every sheet.

Quiet by design
Just the sheet: text on the left, answers on the right, nothing floating over it. Click an answer to copy it; right-click it to copy the line or convert it. Suggestions appear as grey text after the cursor; Tab takes one.

Made for laptops
On Android laptops and in desktop windows your sheets are listed on the left, with keyboard shortcuts for everything. A mini calculator can stay on top of your other windows. Select a sum in any app and choose Calculate to see the answer right there. Export a sheet as PDF, a web page, CSV, Markdown or text, or print it.

Private
No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Your sheets stay on your device. The only thing Summa downloads is public exchange-rate tables, and it never sends anything about you or your sheets.
