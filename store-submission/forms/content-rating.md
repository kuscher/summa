# Content rating (Play Console › Policy › App content › Content rating, IARC questionnaire)

- **Category:** All other app types (reference, news, productivity, tools…), not a game.
- **Violence, fear, sexuality, nudity, crude humour, profanity:** No.
- **Gambling or simulated gambling:** No.
- **Controlled substances (alcohol, tobacco, drugs):** The example sheet for new installs has one
  line item, "Pretzels and beer: 4 × €12.40" (a Munich weekend budget). Answer the alcohol
  question honestly as a passing text reference, not depiction or encouragement. If you'd rather
  avoid the question, change that line in `SummaApp.kt` (for example to "Pretzels and Spezi").
- **Users interact or exchange content with each other:** No.
- **Shares the user's location with others:** No.
- **Allows purchases of digital goods:** No.
- **Unrestricted internet or web browsing:** No (only downloads rate tables).
- **Is it a web browser or search engine:** No.

Expected rating: Everyone / PEGI 3 / USK 0 (possibly with a mild "alcohol reference" note).
