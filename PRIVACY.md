# Summa privacy policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Summa is a notepad calculator made by Alexander Kuscher as a personal hobby project. This policy
covers the Summa app for Android.

## The short version

Summa doesn't collect, store or share any information about you. There are no accounts, ads,
analytics or trackers.

## Your sheets

Your sheets and settings are stored on your device, in Summa's private app storage. If you have
Android backup turned on, Android includes them in your device backup in your own Google account;
that backup is managed by Android and your account, and the developer can't see it. Uninstalling
Summa deletes its data from the device.

## Internet use

Summa uses the internet for one thing: downloading public exchange-rate tables, at most twice a
day and only while Settings › Live exchange rates is on.

- Currency rates come from Frankfurter (frankfurter.dev), with the European Central Bank
  (ecb.europa.eu) as a fallback.
- Crypto prices come from CoinGecko (coingecko.com), only if you turn on Settings › Crypto prices.

These requests ask for the rate tables and nothing else. They contain no sheet content, no account
or device identifier and no personal information. Like any web request, they reach those servers
from your device's internet address; their own privacy policies apply to how they handle requests.
With live rates off, Summa makes no network requests at all.

## Other apps

When you use Share, Export, Print or Calculate, the text or file you choose goes to the app or
service you pick, under that app's own terms.

## Children

Summa isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published in this file, in the Summa source repository
(github.com/kuscher/summa). Questions: open an issue at github.com/kuscher/summa/issues.
