Summa 1.3.4: back on phones and tablets on Google Play.

**Install or update:** download `Summa.apk` below and open it; your sheets stay. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS`.

- **Google Play offers Summa to phones and tablets again.** 1.3.3 limited the Play listing to
  Googlebooks and other desktop-class devices. Summa works well on phones, so that limit is gone.
  Nothing changes in the app itself, and the APK here always installed everywhere.
