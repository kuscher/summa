Summa 1.3.3: on Google Play, Summa is now offered to Googlebooks and other desktop-class Android devices only.

**Install or update:** download `Summa.apk` below and open it; your sheets stay. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS`.

- **Google Play is desktop-only now.** Play shows Summa only on devices that report themselves as a
  PC, such as Googlebooks. Nothing changes in the app itself, and the APK here still installs on
  Android 12 or newer phones and tablets.

Also in this release, from 1.3.2 (which went to Google Play's testers but was never published here):

- **Start typing right away:** with a keyboard attached, the cursor is in the sheet as soon as it
  opens (a new sheet, another sheet from the list, back from Settings). On phones only a new, empty
  sheet takes the cursor, so reading a sheet doesn't pop up the keyboard.
- **Esc does what you'd expect:** in the search field it clears the search, and once more goes back
  to the sheet; in Settings it goes back to the sheet.
- Summa says it doesn't need a touchscreen, so laptops and desktops with only a keyboard and mouse
  or trackpad can install it from Google Play.
